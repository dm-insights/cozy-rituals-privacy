Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026 · App version 1.0.0

The short version. Cozy Rituals keeps everything on your phone. Your rituals, timers and progress are stored on your device and are never sent to us.

There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, sell, rent or share your personal data.

1. Who we are

Cozy Rituals is developed and published by DM Insights ("we", "us", "our"). For the purposes of data protection law, we would be the controller of any personal data we receive, which, as explained below, is only the content of messages you choose to send us.

Contact: cozy_rituals@proton.me

2. What the app stores on your device

To work, the app saves the following in a private database on your phone:

  • the rituals you create (names, icons, default durations, order, optional reminders);
  • your daily plan (which rituals are on Today and for how long);
  • your timer sessions (when a ritual was started, paused or finished, and how much time you spent);
  • your settings (for example, reminder time and whether you finished onboarding).

This information stays on your device. We cannot see it, and the app does not upload it anywhere.

3. What we do not collect

The app does not ask for or collect:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any account details (there are no accounts);
  • your location, contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone data;
  • advertising identifiers or other device identifiers;
  • analytics, usage statistics or crash reports;
  • payment information (the app has no purchases in this version).

The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking tools.

4. Permissions the app asks for

Some features need permissions from your phone's operating system. They are used only on your device, and you can change or withdraw them at any time in your system settings.

Android.

NotificationsTo show your reminders, the running-timer notification and the alert when a ritual's time is up.
Alarms & remindersTo let the end-of-timer alert arrive on time, even when the app is closed or the phone is in silent mode.
Battery optimization exemptionTo stop Android from delaying your timer alerts while the app is in the background.
Foreground service (media playback)To keep the end-of-timer alert sound repeating until you respond, even when the app is in the background. It runs only while that alert is ringing.
VibrationTo vibrate with alerts.
InternetDeclared by the app framework. The app does not use it to send your data to us or to anyone else.

iOS.

NotificationsTo remind you and to tell you when a ritual's time is up. Nothing else is requested on iOS.

The app is designed to work if you decline any of these permissions, although timer alerts and reminders may then be late or missing.

5. Exporting your data

In Settings you can export your rituals, daily plans and sessions as a file. The file is created on your device and handed to the Android share sheet. You decide where it goes (for example, Files, email or a messaging app). We do not receive it. Once you share it with another app or service, that service's own privacy policy applies.

6. Backups made by your device

Android. If you have Google backup turned on for your device, Android may include the app's data in your device backup, which is stored in your Google account and governed by Google's terms and privacy policy. We have no access to that backup. You can manage or delete it in your Google account and Android settings.

iOS. If you have iCloud backup (or another device backup method) turned on, iOS may include the app's local database in that backup, according to your own device settings. That backup is governed by Apple's (or your backup provider's) own terms and privacy policy. We never receive or have access to it. You can manage or delete it in your device's backup settings.

7. Third parties

We do not share your data with third parties, because we do not have it. The app is distributed through Google Play and Apple's App Store. Google and Apple may each process information about your installation and your device, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy. That processing is done by Google or Apple and not by us.

If you email us, your message and email address pass through your email provider and ours.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your data stays on your phone until you remove it.
  • You can remove individual rituals and sessions inside the app.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes its local data from your device. Any Android backup (section 6) or exported file (section 5) is separate, and you control those.
  • Because nothing is stored on our servers, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

9. Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, which Android isolates from other apps. For better protection, use a screen lock on your device and keep it up to date. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but since your data never leaves your device, it is not exposed to risks of servers or network transfer.

10. Children

Cozy Rituals is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Your rights

Everyone. You are in control of your data: you can view, change, export and delete it inside the app, and you can remove it entirely by uninstalling.

European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland. Under the GDPR and similar laws you have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. We do not process personal data from the app, so there is no legal basis to name for it. The only personal data we may receive is what you send us when you contact us (such as your email address and message). We use it solely to reply to you, on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering your request, and we keep it only as long as needed for that purpose. To exercise any right over such correspondence, email us.

United States (including California, Colorado, Virginia and other states). Under laws such as the CCPA/CPRA you may have rights to know, delete and correct personal information and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not collect personal information through the app, and we do not sell it or share it for advertising. We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

The app does not respond to "Do Not Track" signals because it does not track you.

12. International transfers

The app does not transfer your data anywhere. Emails you send us may be processed by email providers in other countries.

13. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, adding cloud backup, accounts or paid features), we will update this policy before the change is released, change the effective date above, and ask for your consent where the law requires it. The latest version is always available at this page.
